QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every workout works with your bodyweight, a backpack or a pair of dumbbells. Gym machines are optional.

Is the advice proven?

Yes. Each step follows published research and guidelines from groups like the American Academy of Dermatology, the WHO and sleep medicine bodies. The plan keeps the language simple and leaves out the heavy study data.

How do I get the PDF?

Right after payment you get the PDF to download, and a copy by email.

What is the difference between tiers?

Glowmaxx is the core 28-day plan. Pro adds the skin, shaving, style and meal guides. Ultimate adds personality scripts and a fillable daily tracker.

What if I miss a day?

Skip it and carry on. Never double up. Missing one day does not matter, quitting does.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is general guidance. See a doctor for skin, health or mental health concerns.
